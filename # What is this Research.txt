# What is this Research?

### Title:

**A Web-Based Industrial Training Management System: A Case Study of the School of Computing**

### In Simple Terms:

This research focuses on developing a **web-based system** to manage and streamline the entire **Industrial Training (Internship) process** for university students.

---

# Why is this System Needed?

Currently, internship-related information is scattered across multiple platforms such as:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* Google Drive
* GitHub
* Printed Documents

As a result:

❌ Students become confused about where to find information.

❌ Students may use outdated documents and forms.

❌ The same questions are asked repeatedly.

❌ Coordinators spend a lot of time answering repetitive inquiries.

To solve these issues, a **centralized web-based platform** is proposed.

---

# What Does This System Do?

The system acts as a **One-Stop Platform** where all industrial training activities can be managed in a single website.

The system includes:

* Student Management
* Document Management
* Announcements
* Questions & Answers
* Downloads
* User Management

---

# Who Are the Users of the System?

## 1. Student

A student who is undertaking industrial training or an internship.

## 2. Training Coordinator

# Viva Presentation Summary

> My Final Year Research Project is a **Web-Based Industrial Training Management System**. Currently, industrial training information is distributed across multiple platforms, which causes confusion among students and increases the workload of coordinators. To address this problem, the proposed system provides a centralized web application where students, training coordinators, and administrators can manage documents, announcements, questions and answers, and other training-related information in a single platform. The system aims to improve information accessibility, reduce administrative workload, enhance communication, and make the industrial training management process more efficient and organized.

### Project Type

This project can be considered an **Internship Management Portal** that provides a centralized environment for managing all industrial training activities. It is a practical solution that can be implemented and used by universities to improve the efficiency of internship management processes.
